Bangladesh exam results and conflict events, 2011–2025

The SSC and HSC district results sheet, one row per district per year, with a new Conflict summary column. Click any row with events to see what happened that year.

* HSC 2020 was not held because of COVID-19. That year's HSC results are automatic passes, so the 100% pass rates are not exam outcomes.

Conflict events come from the UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset (Uppsala Conflict Data Program). Each event is an incident of organized violence; deaths are UCDP's best estimate. Events are matched to districts from their recorded location and matched by calendar year, not by exam date.

Event types follow UCDP: State vs armed group (government forces against an organized armed group), Clashes between groups (non-state groups such as party supporters fighting each other), Attacks on civilians (one-sided violence by a group or the state against unarmed people).
